# AquaFlow Financial Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an end-to-end financial analytics solution built in Power BI for a utility services company. The dashboard was designed to provide executives and finance stakeholders with a clear view of financial performance, profitability, operational efficiency, liquidity, and cash flow management.

The report covers five major business areas:

* Financial Overview
* Profit & Loss Analysis
* Balance Sheet Monitoring
* Income Statement Performance
* Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC)

---

## Business Objective

The goal of this dashboard is to transform financial data into actionable business insights that support strategic decision-making.

Key questions answered include:

* Is revenue growing sustainably?
* Are operational expenses under control?
* Which revenue streams contribute most to profitability?
* How efficiently is the company managing working capital?
* How does actual performance compare to budget and historical results?
* What financial risks and opportunities exist?

---

## Key Findings

### Revenue Performance

The company generated AED 6.84M in revenue, exceeding budget expectations and achieving positive year-over-year growth.

Revenue growth was primarily driven by:

* Water Supply Services
* Sewage & Waste Services
* Industrial Contracts

These segments represent the core drivers of business performance and demonstrate strong market demand.

### Profitability

Gross Profit reached AED 4.63M with a Gross Margin of 67.7%.

Net Profit increased by 12.4% year-over-year, indicating that revenue growth is translating into bottom-line improvement.

This suggests strong operational efficiency and effective cost management.

### Expense Analysis

Although operating expenses increased slightly, profit growth outpaced expense growth.

The largest cost contributors were:

* Operations
* Administration
* Maintenance

Budget variance analysis revealed areas where spending exceeded planned levels and highlighted opportunities for future optimization.

### Balance Sheet Health

The company demonstrated:

* Growth in total assets
* Increasing cash balances
* Strong receivable management
* Stable equity growth

These indicators suggest a financially healthy organization with improving liquidity.

### Cash Flow Efficiency

One of the strongest findings was the Cash Conversion Cycle (CCC).

Current CCC = 12.3 Days

Industry Benchmark = 35 Days

The company significantly outperformed industry standards due to:

* Lower Days Sales Outstanding (DSO)
* Lower Days Inventory Outstanding (DIO)
* Effective supplier payment management

This indicates excellent working capital management and faster cash generation.

---

## Strategic Recommendations

### 1. Expand High-Performing Revenue Streams

Focus investment and sales efforts on:

* Water Supply Services
* Sewage Services
* Industrial Contracts

These segments consistently generate the highest contribution to revenue and profit.

### 2. Strengthen Cost Governance

Implement monthly monitoring of:

* Operations Costs
* Administrative Expenses
* Labor Costs

Automated variance alerts can help management react before budget overruns occur.

### 3. Improve Predictive Planning

Future versions of this dashboard could integrate:

* Revenue Forecasting
* Profit Forecasting
* Cash Flow Forecasting

Using statistical models and Power BI forecasting capabilities.

### 4. Enhance Executive Decision Support

Additional KPIs can be introduced:

* Customer Profitability
* Regional Profitability
* Service Line Profitability
* Budget Achievement %
* EBITDA Trend Analysis

### 5. Continue Working Capital Optimization

The company's CCC performance is a competitive advantage and should remain a strategic focus area.

Maintaining low DSO and inventory turnover efficiency will continue to strengthen liquidity and reduce financing needs.
